ACT I · THE FILM

The film

Life is not a race. Life is a film.
And this film has only one audience: you.

Life is not a race, yaar. Life is a film. And this film has only one audience, and that audience is you! How, you ask? ... You see your life through your own eyes. That makes you the only audience of the film called Life.

ACT III · THE FEAR OF PEOPLE

THE QUESTION THAT CHANGES THE SCENE

WHAT WILL
PEOPLE SAY?

“What will people say?” This is the biggest disease of all: “What will people say?” Out of fear of what people will say, you’re running in life’s race... a race that may not even have been yours! You’re running the wrong race, and you still want to succeed?? You’re thinking, “What will people say?” ... Come on, yaar, people’s job is to talk. They’ll always have something or other to say.

But ask yourself once: whose life is it? Yours or theirs? Then why live according to other people? Write your own story. Become the writer of your own film.

ACT IV · YOUR STORY, YOUR RULES

YOUR SCENE. YOUR WORDS.

WRITE
YOUR OWN
STORY.

And if people have something to say, just tell them this: “Who are you to write my story? This is my story. I’ll do what I want. Some days I’ll be Heer’s Ranjha, some days Roja’s Haalen... One day, the king of the world; the next, a beggar on the street... my story, my choice.”

That’s why, yaar, write your own story. No one can write your story better than you.

ACT V · THE DREAMS

KEEP THIS SCENE IN THE CUT

The
dreams.

And if you chase your dreams, will you be happy? Try chasing them once... the ache of chasing them will start to feel precious. No one will be happier than you.

Think about it before time runs out... because when you regret it, there won’t be time. If you don’t chase your dreams, then one thing is certain: when you grow old and lie in a hospital bed, you’ll think, “If only I’d chased my dreams that day. Maybe I’d be a little happier today.”

This is the time to chase your dreams. To take part in your own race. Choose the right race, and then you’ll see: you’ll come first.

FINAL ACT · THE AUDIENCE

And you know the fun part? Your film won’t flop. Want to know why? Because in this film, you are the director, the writer, and the audience. And every director really likes their own film!

LIFE IS A FILM,
NOT A RACE.
